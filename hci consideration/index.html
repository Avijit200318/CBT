HCI Consideration

  • Consistency & Standards: Uniform layout with bottom-anchored navigation for predictable usability.
  • Aesthetic & Minimalist Design: High-contrast colors and plenty of whitespace to prevent cognitive overload.
  • Recognition Rather Than Recall: Standardized icons (like a house and arrows) that leverage existing user knowledge.
  • User Control & Freedom: Clear, persistent "Home" and "Exit" buttons so users never feel trapped.
